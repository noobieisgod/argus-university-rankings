Acceptance Rate Policy

Prefer each institution's latest available CDS, with reviewed CDS-derived summaries allowed. If acceptance is unavailable, use U.S. News undergraduate acceptance with its own edition/cohort or dated capture. Explicitly label user-supplied rates and unspecified publisher dates. Never label it CDS. No older-CDS field backfill or IPEDS acceptance substitution. Yield remains pooled IPEDS 2022/2023/2024.

Within Prestige: 40% U.S. News history, 30% acceptance score and 30% yield score. SAT and high-school GPA never affect scores. Missing admissions metadata displays as Not Reported on this website without altering the exported evidence state.

Acceptance score

100 * (1 - SQRT(acceptance rate))

Prefer each institution's latest available CDS, with reviewed CDS-derived summaries allowed. If acceptance is unavailable, use U.S. News undergraduate acceptance with its own edition/cohort or dated capture. Explicitly label user-supplied rates and unspecified publisher dates. Never label it CDS. No older-CDS field backfill or IPEDS acceptance substitution. Yield remains pooled IPEDS 2022/2023/2024.

Yield score

100 * SQRT(sum(enrollees) / sum(admits))

Use first-time degree/certificate-seeking undergraduate full-time + part-time enrollees. Pool campus UNITID counts, not percentages. No denominator 0 or enrolled > admitted > applicants.

Historical weighting

Base weights: 60% 2027, 25% 2026, 15% 2025. Use all supported available editions.

Divide weighted score sum by available weight sum. One year gets100%. Current + previous gets70.588235% /29.411765%. No cherry-picking, copying or extrapolation.

Scope and history

National and Regional Universities only. No LAC or Regional Colleges.

Convert each edition in its actual category. National uses2x curve. Regional Universities use frozen log model for ranks1-30. Use every supported observation in the latest three-edition window, minimum one.

Category completeness

Outcomes gate unchanged. Prestige needs supported USN history, latest-CDS or approved U.S. News undergraduate acceptance, and valid pooled IPEDS yield.

Experience and Academic/Learning pair requirements unchanged. No category redistribution. Available-year redistribution is permitted only inside U.S. News history.

Experience first priority

Actual PR + WSJ Recommendation: 56.25% / 43.75%.

Actual PR and WSJ retained whenever valid. No source shopping for a higher score.

Experience second priority

Without PR: Campus + WSJ Recommendation, same weights.

Campus is an independent observed grade. It is not a predicted PR rating.

Experience third priority

Without WSJ Recommendation: actual PR + Campus, same weights.

Both required. Campus alone cannot fill both positions. No Experience redistribution.

Learning first priority

Complete WSJ Opportunities + Facilities: 60% / 40%.

When both scores are valid, Niche does not replace either.

Learning fallback

If either WSJ input is missing, use Niche Academics + Professors: 60% / 40%.

Whole-pair substitution only. No mixed WSJ/Niche pair. Unknown or missing required grade produces NR.

Fixed earnings curve

clamp(50 + 40 * log2(earnings / benchmark), 0, 100)

Apply separately to Scorecard earnings and PayScale Mid-Career Pay, with fixed, stage-appropriate benchmarks. Clamp means limit the score to 0-100. Never derive benchmarks from the ranked schools.

PayScale earnings

2024 report: bachelor-only Mid-Career Pay

Fixed benchmark $87,000 in 2024 dollars; $89,289.20363799814 in 2025 dollars. Age 35-45 national proxy, not an exact experience-matched comparison.

College Scorecard

MD_EARN_WNE_5YR. Use the most recent comparable institution-level cohort.

Keep the measure and release/cohort selection consistent across schools. Use a common inflation-adjusted dollar year.

Published 0-100 values

Use actual published PR and WSJ school-level scores directly.

Do not stretch observed ranges, use percentiles, sample z-scores or selected-school minimum/maximum normalization. A 0-100 source score is not necessarily an absolute measure.

Ties

Sort by unrounded final score. Start each tie group with the highest remaining score and include schools within 0.50 points of it.

The whole group spread must be <=0.50. No chained ties. Use competition ranks, retain exact scores and interpret tiny differences cautiously.

Coverage

Report availability against original metric and edition weights, without redistribution.

Shorter U.S. News history may rank but has lower history coverage. Actual National scores count as direct. Regional estimates count only as usable. No missing-history score penalty.

Regional uncertainty

LOOCV MAE 9.998010, RMSE 13.384303 Prestige points.

At 8% overall: MAE 0.799841, RMSE 1.070744 overall points. Largest observed residual 2.680581 overall points; not a prediction interval or future-error bound.

Regional standing is an experimental frozen estimate. Its documented validation limitations remain visible; it is not a published National University rank.

STEM Ranking Methodology

A school must have an engineering and CS rank either through the primary or fallback source to be ranked.

  • USN Graduate EngineeringApproved backup: Niche engineering30%
  • U.S. News Best Computer Science SchoolsApproved backup: Niche Computer Science30%
  • U.S. News ChemistryApproved backup: Niche chemistry10%
  • U.S. News MathematicsApproved backup: Niche mathematics10%
  • U.S. News PhysicsApproved backup: Niche physics10%
  • U.S. News Biological SciencesApproved backup: Niche Biology10%

Engineering and CS are required through primary or approved fallback. Renormalize across available components, then subtract 2 points for each of Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics or Biology missing from both sources, up to 8 points. A valid fallback provides full component coverage.

The accepted limitation: a sufficiently weak observed science score can produce a lower result than complete absence. This is a modest breadth deduction, not a no-credit fixed denominator.

Business Ranking Methodology

Owned qualifying degree unit and designated publisher rank required. No fallback.

  • U.S. News Best Business SchoolsNo fallback100%

Owned qualifying business unit and a usable U.S. News full-time MBA rank are required. Undergraduate business and Niche Business are not scoring inputs.

Law Ranking Methodology

Owned qualifying degree unit and designated publisher rank required. No fallback.

  • U.S. News Best Law SchoolsNo fallback100%

Princeton Review Career ratings have no active scoring role. LawHub employment fields are contextual only and never affect the score.

Medical Ranking Methodology

Owned qualifying degree unit and designated publisher rank required. No fallback.

  • Admit MD/DO MedicalNo fallback100%

Both MD and DO schools qualify. Use the defensible main campus; where multiple usable ranks genuinely map to that campus or indivisible institution, average the published ranks, then transform the average. Preserve every observation. Unranked entries do not enter the mean.

Ranks, scores, and source conventions

ARGUS ranks and publisher ranks are different. Single-source specialties preserve a publisher ordering through a fixed score conversion; ARGUS tie groups can combine nearby scores. Numerical rank bands use their arithmetic midpoint. Legitimate zero scores are covered; missing values are not zero.

Specialty rank scores use the fixed anchors below, interpolated linearly and clamped to zero after #2000. Overall U.S. News standing uses its separate steeper curve, not this specialty scale.

Provenance & limitations

Verified export: schema 2, 237 institutions. Source citations are provided without publisher input tables. Publisher snapshots can be dated differently; this is not a real-time admissions feed.

The source workbook hash and exact dataset hashes are retained in the local handoff report. Normal website builds read JSON only. The underlying rankings are not changed by this website.

Underlying data requests

This public build contains ARGUS results, institutional admissions facts, campus locations and source citations. Publisher input ranks, ratings, component tables, research extracts and original datasets are not distributed.

Request a reproducibility review or permitted data access. Identify the schools, fields and intended use. Do not post personal information, credentials or restricted source material in a public issue. Requests are reviewed individually; third-party data is supplied only where redistribution is permitted. Otherwise, source links and access instructions are provided. A request does not guarantee access or grant a reuse license.

Reducing public exposure does not itself establish permission for derived rankings. The publication review remains a separate launch requirement.

Privacy & analytics

The public website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits and performance. The beacon sends page and referrer information, browser/device information and timing measurements to Cloudflare. Cloudflare states that this service does not use cookies or local storage, fingerprint visitors, or retain visitor IP addresses. Query strings are not logged.

ARGUS sends no custom events, search text, school selections or comparison choices to analytics. Sorting and filtering do not create additional page views. Analytics is disabled on local development origins. Browser protections can prevent measurement, so totals are approximate. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages under GitHub’s privacy statement.
